# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the enquiry to find the statement of the account?
A: STMT.ENT.BOOK is the enquiry used to find and view the statement of the account.

Q: What is the enquiry used to view all accounts, loans, deposits, and arrangements?
A: LBK.CUSTOMER.POSITION is the enquiry used to view all accounts, loans, deposits, and arrangements. It displays the list of the client's products, including cash-collateral deposits.
